# Twilight Run sound recordings

- `collision.mp3` excerpts [Car accident. Real. Interior.](https://freesound.org/people/softwalls/sounds/449062/) by softwalls, an in-car recording of an actual rear impact. Trimmed around the impact, filtered, faded and gain-adjusted.
- `debris.mp3` excerpts [Scrap metal dropping / crashing](https://freesound.org/people/SamsterBirdies/sounds/587443/) by SamsterBirdies, a recording of loose scrap hitting concrete. Trimmed, filtered, faded and gain-adjusted.
- `horn.mp3` excerpts [Car Horn Honk.wav](https://freesound.org/people/DeVern/sounds/349922/) by DeVern, a stationary car horn recording. Trimmed to a single honk, filtered, faded and gain-adjusted.

The recordings were released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
